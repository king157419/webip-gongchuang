# Character C — 「通通」Tong (the signal bird / messenger)
Concept: a round little bird messenger — ties to the School of Communication & Information Engineering (signals, messages) and to the club's role of spreading AI knowledge and news ("通通快报").
- Body: a perfectly round chubby ball-bird (like a fluffy sparrow chick) — single round mass, tiny stubby wings fused to the sides, two short orange feet with flat soles so it stands.
- Signature crest: on top of its head, a crest shaped like a Wi-Fi / signal-wave symbol (three nested arcs), made as ONE solid chunky fin (arcs joined, print-friendly), coloured coral-orange.
- Face: big round shiny eyes, tiny orange triangular beak, rosy cheeks.
- Accessory: a small round "message bubble" shaped badge on its chest in cobalt blue, OR a tiny cobalt messenger satchel strap (choose one, keep it simple and fused).
- Palette: warm cream-white #FFF6E8 body, coral-orange #FF7A45 crest/beak/feet, cobalt blue #2F5BEA badge/accent, light grey-beige #E9DCC8 belly shading.
- Personality: chatty, cheerful, always first with the news; loves sharing what it learns.
