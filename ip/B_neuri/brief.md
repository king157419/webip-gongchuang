# Character B — 「元元」Neuri (a neuron that learns)
Concept: a cute neuron cell / "neural node". Every time it learns something, it lights up a new connection — the club's spirit of learning together.
- Body: a round soft mochi/dumpling-shaped body (single mass, slightly squashed sphere, flat bottom so it stands).
- Dendrites as limbs: two stubby rounded "arms" and two stubby "feet" that are soft nubs; plus ONE short thick curl on top of the head (like a sprout/cowlick) ending in a small glowing round node — this is its signature "spark". All chunky, fused to the body.
- Small glowing dot-nodes: 3 tiny round nodes in a warm yellow embedded on the body like freckles/constellation, connected by subtle raised lines (embossed, not separate parts).
- Face: two big simple glossy black oval eyes with white highlights, small happy mouth, soft pink cheeks.
- Palette: mint-teal #5ED6C1 body, deep teal #127C74 accents/shadows, warm yellow #FFD54A glowing nodes, soft pink #FF9AA8 cheeks, cream-white belly patch #F6FFF9.
- Personality: curious, enthusiastic beginner who never stops asking "why?" — represents new members learning AI.
