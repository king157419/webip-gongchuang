# Character C — 「通通」Tong (the signal bird / messenger)
Concept: a round little bird messenger — ties to the School of Communication & Information Engineering (signals, messages) and to the club's role of spreading AI knowledge and news ("通通快报").
- Body: a perfectly round chubby ball-bird (like a fluffy sparrow chick) — single round mass, tiny stubby wings fused to the sides, two short orange feet with flat soles so it stands.
- Signature crest: on top of its head, a crest shaped like a Wi-Fi / signal-wave symbol (three nested arcs), made as ONE solid chunky fin (arcs joined, print-friendly), coloured coral-orange.
- Face: big round shiny eyes, tiny orange triangular beak, rosy cheeks.
- Accessory: a small round "message bubble" shaped badge on its chest in cobalt blue, OR a tiny cobalt messenger satchel strap (choose one, keep it simple and fused).
- Palette: warm cream-white #FFF6E8 body, coral-orange #FF7A45 crest/beak/feet, cobalt blue #2F5BEA badge/accent, light grey-beige #E9DCC8 belly shading.
- Personality: chatty, cheerful, always first with the news; loves sharing what it learns.
## Context
Mascot (IP character) for the 上海大学人工智能社 (Shanghai University AI Club, hosted by the School of Communication & Information Engineering). Audience: university students. Uses: club website, WeChat posts, recruitment posters, stickers, merch, and a small **3D-printed figurine** (FDM/resin, ~6-8 cm tall).

## Hard design rules (apply to EVERY image)
- Chibi proportions, head:body about 1:1 or one rounded single-mass body; very cute, friendly, soft rounded forms.
- 3D-print friendly: chunky simple silhouette, NO thin wires, NO floating parts, NO long thin antennae/fingers, stubby limbs fused to body, flat-ish feet so it stands on its own, max ~5 distinct parts.
- Limited palette (3-4 colours) exactly as specified below; the SAME character design in every image (same colours, same features, same proportions).
- NO text, letters, logos or watermarks inside any image (I will add real typography myself). Leave clean empty space where noted.
- Original design: do NOT imitate any existing famous character (no Pop Mart Labubu/Molly, no Pokemon, no Sanrio, no Doraemon, no Baymax, no EVE/WALL-E).
