# Character A — 「小智」Zhi (evolution of the club's existing logo)
The club's current logo is a glossy sky-blue egg-shaped robot head with two antennae ending in balls, big dark-blue headphone discs on both sides, and a dark navy rounded-rectangle visor "face screen" showing pixel eyes (one eye is a pixel "X/plus", the other a pixel dash). The reference logo is attached.
Evolve it into a full mascot while keeping instant recognisability:
- Body: one smooth egg/bean shape (head and body as ONE mass, slightly wider at the bottom), two tiny stubby rounded arms, two short round feet.
- Face: the navy rounded visor screen with glowing cyan PIXEL eyes — make the pixel eyes cute (rounded pixel shapes; they change per expression: ^ ^ happy, ? thinking, stars celebrating, - - sleepy). Add a tiny pixel blush on the screen.
- Headphones: chunky cylinder discs on both sides (print-friendly).
- Antennae: SHORT and THICK (stubby nubs with ball tips, like little bean sprouts), not thin wires.
- Small detail: a tiny round "chest light" button in cyan.
- Palette: sky blue #4FC3F7 body (soft gradient ok), deep cobalt #1565C0 headphones/antennae, navy #1A1F5C screen, cyan #00E5FF pixel glow, white highlights.
- Personality: earnest, helpful senior club member; "your study buddy".
## Context
Mascot (IP character) for the 上海大学人工智能社 (Shanghai University AI Club, hosted by the School of Communication & Information Engineering). Audience: university students. Uses: club website, WeChat posts, recruitment posters, stickers, merch, and a small **3D-printed figurine** (FDM/resin, ~6-8 cm tall).

## Hard design rules (apply to EVERY image)
- Chibi proportions, head:body about 1:1 or one rounded single-mass body; very cute, friendly, soft rounded forms.
- 3D-print friendly: chunky simple silhouette, NO thin wires, NO floating parts, NO long thin antennae/fingers, stubby limbs fused to body, flat-ish feet so it stands on its own, max ~5 distinct parts.
- Limited palette (3-4 colours) exactly as specified below; the SAME character design in every image (same colours, same features, same proportions).
- NO text, letters, logos or watermarks inside any image (I will add real typography myself). Leave clean empty space where noted.
- Original design: do NOT imitate any existing famous character (no Pop Mart Labubu/Molly, no Pokemon, no Sanrio, no Doraemon, no Baymax, no EVE/WALL-E).

## Deliverables — use your image_gen tool, then save each final PNG INTO THIS FOLDER with exactly these filenames
